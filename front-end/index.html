Sialkot, Punjab — On The Factory Floor

From Sialkot's Factory Floor

Sialkot, Pakistan has built the world's sports-goods for well over a century. Sabri Trader runs full supply-chain integration here — playing kits for any sport, outerwear like puffer and softshell jackets, and everyday casual and team wear, all cut, sewn, decorated, and packed under one roof.

Most overseas buyers dealing with Pakistani sportswear never see a factory at all — they deal with a trading office that quotes a price, then places the actual order with whichever workshop is free that month. Sabri Trader was built the other way around: production stays on our own cutting and stitching lines, so the person who quotes your order is the same operation accountable for it landing on time, in spec, and in the sizing you approved.

That setup is also why the product list here isn't fenced off to one sport or one garment type: the same pattern-making and stitching discipline that holds up on a hockey kit carries over to a rain jacket or a private-label streetwear run, so a club or distributor with programs across several categories can consolidate all of it onto one production line instead of juggling several suppliers.

Common Questions

What's the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the garment type, fabric, and how much customization is involved — a simple single-color tee runs differently than a fully sublimated, multi-panel jersey. Send your spec through the contact form and we'll quote a minimum against that exact order, not a generic factory floor.

Can you handle more than one sport in a single order?

Yes. If your club or distributor account runs programs across several sports, or mixes playing kits with outerwear and casual lines, we can consolidate all of it into one production and shipping schedule instead of running separate suppliers for each category.

How long does sampling and production take?

Pre-production samples typically ship within days of a confirmed tech pack and fabric selection; bulk production timelines depend on order volume and current factory load. We'll give you a firm date range once your spec and quantity are confirmed, and hold to it.

Do you offer private labeling and bespoke cuts?

Yes. Your woven labels, care labels, hangtags, and packaging go on the garment, and we'll adjust the pattern block to a bespoke fit rather than resizing a stock template — nothing ships under the Sabri Trader name unless you ask for it to.

What are your payment terms?

Standard terms are a deposit to confirm production with the balance due before shipment, though this can flex for repeat accounts. We'll lay out exact terms in your quote so there's nothing to guess at.

Which countries do you ship to?

We regularly route air and sea freight to the EU, UK, US, and APAC, packed and documented to commercial import standard. If you're outside those regions, ask — we can usually still route it.

Figures above are typical ranges. Every quote is confirmed in writing against your specific order before production starts.
